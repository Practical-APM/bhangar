1. What Bhangar.Space Is

Bhangar.Space is a hyper-local bulletin board and community discovery platform designed for residents of apartment complexes and residential communities. It allows neighbors to post pre-loved furniture, home appliances, work-from-home setups, and decor when moving in, moving out, or decluttering.

Our Role: Bhangar.Space is not an online store, retailer, broker, logistics company, or payment intermediary. We do not manufacture, inspect, own, store, pack, ship, or deliver any listed items. Every deal, inspection, and payment takes place directly and independently between the buyer and the seller.

When offering items on Bhangar.Space, sellers agree to:

  • Provide honest and truthful descriptions of the item's condition, including any scratches, broken parts, missing accessories, or functional issues.

  • List realistic prices and promptly mark items as sold or delete the listing once a deal has been concluded.
