Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Bhangar.Space is a community platform designed to help residents and moving tenants in apartment complexes and residential societies buy, sell, or pass on pre-loved household items to their neighbors.

We believe privacy policies should be easy to read and completely truthful about what happens with your data. This page clearly describes what information we collect, why we need it, how it is used, and the control you have over your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to let you use the marketplace and communicate with neighbors:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we receive basic authentication details provided by Google OAuth: your full name, email address, and profile picture URL. We use this strictly to verify resident identity and protect the community from spam or automated bots.
  • Resident Profile Details: You may provide your residential apartment society name, your tower or wing, a username, and an optional contact phone number.
  • Item Listings: Photos, titles, prices, descriptions, and condition notes of items you choose to list for sale.
  • Buyer & Seller Offers: When you submit or negotiate an offer on an item, the offer details and messages are recorded to coordinate the handover.
What we never collect: We do not access your Google contacts, emails, Google Drive files, or any private data outside of the basic public profile and email permission granted during sign-in.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to deliver the service:

  • To display your listings to neighbors in your residential community or neighboring societies.
  • To enable direct coordination between buyers and sellers for elevator handovers and in-person pickups.
  • To verify that listings come from real individuals rather than commercial spammers.
  • To save your bookmarks, item drafts, and negotiations.

3. Google User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Bhangar.Space's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Minimal Access: We only access your name, email address, and profile photo provided through Google OAuth to verify resident identity and secure your account.
  • No Advertising Use: We never sell, transfer, or use your Google user data to serve personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertisements.
  • No AI Model Training: We never use or transfer information received from Google APIs to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Unauthorized Transfers: We do not transfer your personal information to third parties, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • Limited Human Access: Human access to your Google user data is strictly limited to technical support, security maintenance, or complying with valid legal obligations.

4. Information Shared Between Neighbors

Bhangar.Space is built for local, in-person pickups. When you list an item, other residents can see the item photos, price, tower name, and your chosen display name.

If a buyer and seller agree to take a deal forward, contact phone numbers may be displayed to each party so they can call or WhatsApp each other to schedule a convenient time to meet for elevator or lobby handover.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, phone numbers, or email addresses to brokers, marketing agencies, or third-party advertisers. We hate spam as much as you do.

6. Payments and Financial Information

Bhangar.Space does not process payments or collect bank account, credit card, or UPI PIN details. All payments are completed directly and offline between the buyer and seller in person (such as via direct UPI transfer or cash upon inspecting the item).

7. Data Storage and Security

Your account details and listings are stored on secure cloud database servers hosted by Supabase (PostgreSQL) using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and row-level security controls.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your content and personal data:

  • You can edit, mark as sold, or delete any of your listings at any time directly through the app.
  • You can sign out of your account at any moment.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by sending an email to our support contact below. We will permanently delete your records within 7 business days.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage (localStorage) to remember your selected residential society, saved bookmarks, and active session so you don't have to re-select your tower every time you visit the page. We do not use third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make meaningful changes to how user information is handled, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
