# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are young working corporate professionals, tech workers, and families aged 21 to 55 living in high-rise residential gated societies across Indian metros (Gurugram, Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Noida).
- **Sellers:** Tenants vacating their apartments or upgrading their homes who need to quickly liquidate non-clothing items (furniture, appliances, electronics, decor) without dealing with stranger spam, packing costs, or security deposit deductions.
- **Buyers:** Newly arriving or resident tenants seeking quality pre-owned furniture, study desks, electronics, and home essentials without paying high retail prices, rental lock-ins, or elevator shifting fees.

## Product Purpose

SocietyDrop is a hyperlocal community marketplace designed for high-rise gated societies. It enables verified apartment neighbors to buy, sell, and negotiate pre-owned electronics, furniture, appliances, and home decor with zero friction, zero listing fees, and immediate elevator/lobby handover.

## Positioning

Unlike generic platforms (OLX, Quikr) that expose residents to off-campus strangers and spam, or chaotic WhatsApp society groups where listings are banned or buried, SocietyDrop offers verified in-building trust:
- Transactions take place within the same gated society or adjacent tower cluster using building luggage trolleys.
- Structured in-app price negotiation and temporary 45-minute inspection holds eliminate ghosting.
- Contact privacy shielding prevents unwanted phone calls and external leaks.

## Operating Context

- Mobile and laptop web interface used by residents in their apartments, elevators, or building lobbies.
- High turnover rental cycles (11-month leases) in metro tech corridors with strict society move-in/move-out rules and elevator shifting permissions.
- In-person inspection at building lobbies or doorstep before final direct UPI payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Scope:** Strictly non-clothing categories (Electronics & WFH gear, Appliances, Furniture, Home Decor & Balcony Plants, Fixer-Uppers / DIY electronics).
- **Core Capabilities:**
  - Fast, frictionless item posting with condition grading and video proof tags.
  - In-app silent price negotiation with structured counter-offers.
  - 45-minute exclusive inspection hold to eliminate neighbor ghosting.
  - In-app privacy-shielded chat with automated masking of personal phone numbers and UPI IDs.
  - Integrated inter-tower luggage trolley assistance and nearby Porter mini-truck bookings.
  - Direct doorstep scrap and e-waste recycling buyout for non-repairable electronics.
- **Tone & Terminology:** Professional, clean, polished English. Strictly no gimmicks, no Hinglish, and no lecturing copy.
- **Visual Language:** High-clarity light theme (slate neutrals, crisp typography, clean surfaces, high contrast, zero neon/glassmorphism).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** SocietyDrop
- **Voice:** Clean, modern, trustworthy, efficient.
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist, crisp, functional, responsive across phone and desktop viewports.

## Evidence on Hand

- Production-grade React/TypeScript frontend with responsive layout and functional negotiation modals.
- Real metro society data profiles (DLF Phase 5 Gurugram, Prestige Shantiniketan Bengaluru, Amanora Pune, ATS Greens Noida, Hiranandani Mumbai).

## Product Principles

1. **Zero-Friction Liquidation:** Listing is 100% free; creating a listing takes under 30 seconds.
2. **Elevator-Level Hyperlocal:** Transactions stay within the gated perimeter; zero outside strangers entering towers.
3. **Respect Resident Time & Privacy:** Asynchronous offers and temporary inspection holds replace disruptive phone calls and ghosting.
4. **Clean, Honest Functionality:** Clear prices, video proof of working condition, and no unnecessary UI fluff.
